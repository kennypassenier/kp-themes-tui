## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-20

The first release. kp-tui carries the twenty-two kp-themes registers into a
terminal: the palettes as generated data, the anatomy of what a terminal can
hold of a theme, and widgets that take a theme rather than naming a colour.

**What it is.**

- **`kp-tui-palette`** — the twenty-two palettes, generated by kp-themes and
  vendored byte for byte from the 7.1.0 release asset `tui/palette.rs`
  (`a134c689b23a5b63…`).
  `vendor/PIN` carries the version, the URL and the checksum that release
  published, and the gates compare the bytes at every commit. No judgement
  lives in this crate; a consumer that only wants the colours takes it and
  stops.
- **`kp-tui`** — the anatomy (`anatomy.rs`: what each register does with a
  border, a header, a selection, a progress track, a cursor, a texture, a
  sweep, an alarm), the theme (`Theme::ink` finds an ink that reads on the
  plate it lands on, `Theme::ramp` reads between a register's five chart
  colours), the colour depth ladder (true colour, 256, 16) and the widgets.
- **`kp-compare`** — not published: the proof tool. It drives homelab's own
  client in a pseudo-terminal, replays both sides through one emulator and
  writes the pages this project was measured with.

**Widgets.** Badge, Choice, Column, CommandPalette, DataTable, Density,
Facts, Field, KeyHints, LogPane, Meter, Popup, SelectList, Spark, Stepper,
Stream, Surface, Ticker, Panel, Rail, Button, and the effects (Glitch,
Spinner, Stage, Strike, Sweep). Every one of them reads its colours, its
shapes and its motion from the register; none names a colour of its own, and
a test walks all 22 registers to say so.

**The proof.** Eight of homelab's nine screens are rebuilt on nothing but
this crate and put side by side with homelab's own client:
[docs/HOMELAB_PROOF.md](docs/HOMELAB_PROOF.md). The first five rebuilds
found ten gaps and a defect between them, the two after that two more
gaps, and every one is closed in the crate rather than worked around in a
screen [docs/CORRECTIONS.md].

**The rules the screens are held to**, all four found by Kenny on the
pictures and all four code now:

- fix-64 — elements begin at fixed points. `label_column` measures what a
  group of labels needs, once; `Meter`, `Field` and `Choice` take it, and a
  log line's host and unit columns come from the buffer.
- fix-65 — a rebuilt screen does everything the original did. The log pane's
  selector really filters, and scrolling back to the tail resumes following.
- fix-66 — a bar wears the weave its own register declares for the ground,
  so no two grounds share a bar.
- fix-67 — sideways scrolling sits on keys azerty does not move.

64 tests, clippy clean at `-D warnings`, fmt clean; CI runs all three on
every push.
